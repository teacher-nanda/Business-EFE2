📐 Grammar

Three meanings of “used to”

Click a sentence, then click the box where the highlighted words belong.

used to + infinitive
a regular habit or state in the past

get used to
the process of becoming familiar with something

be used to
being familiar with something

💭 Check your understanding

  • “Staff used to eat lunch at their desks.” Do they still do it? No. It was a habit in the past, but it is not true now.
  • “It took a while to get used to the commute.” Is the commute strange now? No. At first it was strange, but the speaker became familiar with it.

How we build them

used to + infinitive

PositiveStaff used to eat lunch at their desks.
NegativeWe didn't use to have so much construction.
QuestionDid you use to do everything by hand?

In questions and negatives, there is no “d” after “use.”

get used to / be used to

get used toI will get used to the commute / commuting.
be used toI am used to early mornings / waking up early.

After “get used to” and “be used to,” use a noun or a gerund (“-ing”).

Workplace advice

It's good to talk

Small talk—chatting about trivial topics such as the weather

“Morning, Sammy. Did you see the game last night?” This kind of small talk happens in every office around the world, every day. People who make an effort to talk to others are more well-liked by their colleagues. When you make small talk, you make the other person feel more relaxed, and form a connection with that person. People who are good at small talk tend to be quick thinkers, and businesses like employees who can think on their feet.

So what are the key skills you need to master to be good at small talk? Make eye contact with the other person, and listen. Be interested in what they have to say. Stick to topics such as hobbies, books, films, and the weather. And avoid uncomfortable topics such as politics, religion, and money.

⭐ Lesson Review

Lesson summary

⚙️

Used to + verb

Past habits or situations that are not true now.

I used to work weekends.

In questions and negatives, drop the -d:

Did you use to work in Paris?

We didn't use to have so many meetings.

☕

Be used to / get used to

Be used to = it is normal for you now.

I'm used to working from home.

Get used to = it is becoming normal.

It took a while to get used to the weather.

After to, use a noun or -ing.

🧩

Small talk

Keep it light and friendly.

Safe topics: hobbies, books, films, the weather.

Terrible weather today, isn't it?

Have you seen any good films lately?

Ask a follow-up question to keep the conversation going.

Your turnTell me about two changes in your working life, one from the past and one you are still getting used to.
