📚 Vocabulary

Money and finance (1 of 2)

Click the speaker to hear each phrase. Read the example sentence.

📈
income
money coming into a business
The company's income fell last year.
🧾
expenditure / outlay
an amount of money spent
The initial expenditure on technology was huge, but now we can work faster.
💵
a budget
the amount of money that is available to spend on something
We have a large budget for this movie, so the effects will be amazing.
📉
to get into debt
to get into a situation where you owe people money
We must do all we can to avoid getting into debt.
🏦
an overdraft
extra money the bank allows you to spend
The bank charges for overdrafts now.
🏷️
a profit margin
the difference between the cost of making or buying something and what it's sold for
The profit margin on these T-shirts is huge!
⚖️
to break even
to earn just enough to cover the costs of producing a product
We need to sell two cars to break even.
🔻
to make a loss
to lose money by spending more than you earn
We haven't sold enough pineapples. We've made a loss.
📒
accounts
records of money paid into and out of a business
We need to make sure the accounts are always up to date.
👥
overheads
the regular costs of running a business, such as wages
High overheads make this business difficult to run.
📚 Vocabulary

Money and finance (2 of 2)

Click the speaker to hear each phrase. Read the example sentence.

💲
cost (US) / cost price (UK)
a sales price that covers the costs of producing an item without making a profit
I'm selling these earrings at cost to attract customers.
🔄
cash flow
the rate at which money comes into and goes out of a business
Our clients need to pay now or we will have a cash flow problem.
⛰️
to peak
to reach the highest point
The prices peaked in June, but they're down now.
🚪
to go out of business
to no longer be able to exist as a business
It's always hard to see a company go out of business.
⬇️
to drop
to fall, especially in worth or value
When the CEO left, the company's value dropped.
🛒
to undercut competitors
to charge less than others who sell the same goods or services as you
We need to undercut our competitors or we will lose customers.
💱
the exchange rate
the amount of one currency that you get when you change it for another
Changes in the exchange rate make the market uncertain.
📊
sales figures
the amount or value of total sales over a particular period
Our sales figures have improved consistently each year.
🌦️
an upturn in the market
a change to more positive business conditions
Thankfully, there was an upturn in the market.
🌧️
an economic downturn
a major decline in economic activity
Everyone suffered because of the economic downturn last year.

💭 Check your understanding

  • If you break even, do you make a profit? No. You earn just enough to cover your costs.
  • If a company goes out of business, can it still sell products? No. It is no longer able to exist as a business.
