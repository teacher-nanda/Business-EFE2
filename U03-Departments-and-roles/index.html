Speaking break: departments

Answer the questions about the departments you just learned.

1Which departments does your company have? Name three and say what each one does.
We have Marketing, Sales, and IT. Marketing promotes our products.

I work with Human Resources most, because they handle hiring.
3Which department would you like to visit for a day? Why?
I'd like to visit Research and Development. I want to see new products.

Which department do you think is the busiest? I think it's Sales.

📚 Vocabulary

Describing roles

Click the speaker to hear each phrase.

🏬
to work for
[to be employed by a company]
We all work for a large department store.
🎈
to work in
[to be employed in a department or area of an industry]
I work in event management.
👗
to work as
[to have a particular job or role]
She works as a fashion designer.
💵
to look after
[to ensure something runs smoothly]
He looks after our salaries and wages.
🧰
to be responsible for
[to have the duty of ensuring something is done effectively]
They are responsible for office maintenance.
🗄️
to be in charge of
[to have control and authority over something]
I'm in charge of administration.

💭 Check your understanding

  • “I work in event management.” Is event management the company or the area? The area of an industry. For the company we say “work for.”
  • “She works as a fashion designer.” What does “works as” tell us? Her job or role.

💬 Speaking

Speaking break: describe your job

Use the phrases from this lesson to talk about your own job.

1Use “work for” and “work in”: where do you work?
I work for a travel company. I work in the sales department.
2Use “work as”: what is your job?
I work as a project manager.
3Use “in charge of” or “responsible for”: what do you manage or look after?
I'm in charge of the budget. I'm responsible for the schedule.
4Now ask about someone else's job, using the same phrases.
Where do you work? Who looks after the accounts?

💬 Speaking

Org chart chat

Read the conversation as it goes.
When it is your turn, say what you could say.

TomHi, I'm Tom. It's my first day.
Which department do you work in?
YouSay which department you work in and what your job is.
I work in Marketing. I work as a brand manager.
TomGreat.
Who is in charge of Human Resources?
YouSay who is in charge of HR. Invent a name if you like.
Sofia is in charge of HR. She's very helpful.
TomAnd who do I go to if my computer breaks?
YouTell Tom which department is responsible.
You should contact IT. They're responsible for all our equipment.
TomWhat does the Facilities department do?
YouExplain what Facilities does.
Facilities looks after the office: cleaning, repairs, and security.
TomWho is the CEO? What is the CEO like?
YouDescribe the CEO of your company, or of a company you know.
Our CEO is Marta Lindqvist. She's friendly, and she works very hard.
TomThanks, this is really helpful.
Which department should I visit for a day to learn more?
YouSuggest a department and say why.
I'd visit Sales. They work with every other department.
