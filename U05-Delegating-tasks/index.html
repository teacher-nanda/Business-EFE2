📐 Grammar

How strong is it?

Certain modal verbs can be used to say that someone needs to do something. Click a sentence, then click the box where it belongs.

Obligation
someone needs to do something

No obligation
there is no need to do it

Prohibited
someone must not do it

💭 Check your understanding

  • “You don't have to deal with this today.” Can I deal with it today if I want to? Yes. It is allowed, but it is not necessary.
  • “You must not go into the testing area.” Is it allowed? No. “Must not” means that something is prohibited.
  • Why can “must” sound impolite? It is a direct way to say something needs to be done.
📐 Grammar

How we build them

“Must” does not change with the subject, but “have to” becomes “has to” in the third person singular. Both are followed by the base form of the main verb. “Need” acts like a modal verb here, expressing strong obligation.

📐 Grammar

Sound polite

Other modal verbs can also be used in business to delegate tasks politely.

We have to finish this project soon.

To maintain a friendly, polite atmosphere, use “we” instead of “you” to express obligation.

Could / Would you look after this for me?

“Would” is more formal and is rarely used.

💭 Check your understanding

  • Who has to finish the project when I say “We have to finish this project soon”? Probably you and I, but the speaker is asking more politely.
  • “Could you look after this for me?” Is it an order or a request? A polite request.

HW4 — A problem shared

Daily Office Tips

A problem shared… can be a problem halved

Getting your fellow team members involved in your daily tasks makes life easier for everyone, surely? But only when you know how to delegate effectively. In my experience, I've found it helps if you think about these four simple steps:

1 You need to think about who you're delegating to. Are they the best person for the job? What will they give, and what will they learn?

2 You don't have to follow your team's every step or decision. But you should be communicative and offer advice. A supported team is an effective team.

3 You must set a clear deadline. Everyone needs to know when the project should end. Otherwise your project will lose its momentum.

4 You have to offer your team feedback. Everyone appreciates credit for success, but they also want to know what went wrong.
